Rime Creep

A full-bleed pane of window frost growing in real time: dendritic ice ferns advance from nucleation points on a fixed 60-degree lattice, sprout side branches, thicken behind their own tips and stop dead where two grains collide, leaving the clear glass between them slowly closing over before the whole pane thaws and seeds again.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
