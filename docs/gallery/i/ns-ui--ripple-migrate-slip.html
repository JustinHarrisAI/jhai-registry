Ripple Migrate Slip

A full-bleed dune-ripple field built on real aeolian bedform migration: each ripple's gentle windward face keeps piling higher until it crosses the angle of repose, its steep lee face avalanches, and the crest kicks one step downwind. Many ripples do this on independent, staggered clocks, so the whole train visibly creeps at rest with no two slips in sync.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
