Loader Die Tumble

An ambient loading glyph shaped as a die tumbling face over face on a fixed axle: each 90deg landing overshoots by 8deg and corrects on a spring, with a floor shadow compressing and flaring in sync, rather than rotating cleanly to a stop.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
