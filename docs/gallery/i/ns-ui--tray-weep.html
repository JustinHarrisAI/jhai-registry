Tray Weep

A full-bleed section-divider band built on a bubble-cap distillation column tray: a stack of trays froths continuously as vapor bubbles up through submerged caps, liquid creeps toward a weir and spills to the tray below, and any cap running short on vapor visibly weeps liquid back down through itself instead of bubbling.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
