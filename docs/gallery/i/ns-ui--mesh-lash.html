Mesh Lash

A currency amount field where two meshed gears are the FX quote itself: tooth ratio draws the exchange rate, and mechanical backlash (the dead angle a reversed gear turns through before the other flank engages) is the provider's spread, printed as a currency figure straight off the gear math.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
