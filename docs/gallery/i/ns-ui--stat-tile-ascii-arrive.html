Stat Tile ASCII Arrive

A KPI number whose arrival is ink condensing into a glyph: every dot of a 5x7 ASCII dot-matrix digit starts at a random density-ramp step and eases independently toward its true value, so the numeral visibly resolves out of noise instead of sliding or flipping into place.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
