Wind Regulator Bellows

A streaming media buffer-ahead strip modelled on a pipe organ's wind supply: a feeder bellows pumps discrete strokes into a reservoir whose weighted lid smooths that into a steady reading, and a spring-loaded spill valve bleeds off the excess whenever the lid threatens to overshoot, replacing the flat grey "buffered" overlay most players use.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
