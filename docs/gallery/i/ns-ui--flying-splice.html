Flying Splice

A logo ribbon whose real subject is the roll stand feeding it: one paper roll unwinds at constant web speed, so its radius falls to 0.426 of full while its RPM more than doubles (2.35x — 0.61 to 1.43 rev/s at the demo's 672x220 band), then rebuilds along the exact time-reverse of the same law and slows back down. An unbroken 22s oscillation with no reset, no pop and no second roll.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
