Extrusion Die Cut

A card-scale processing/loader widget modelled on pasta die-face cutting: a textured rope continuously extrudes from a die aperture and a guillotine blade sweeps across it every ~2.2s, severing off a segment that falls and settles into a loose floor stack. The loop is unbounded and shows something always extruding and something periodically finished, without a single digit or bar ever drawn.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
