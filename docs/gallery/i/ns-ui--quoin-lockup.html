Quoin Lockup

A bento grid whose only variable is pressure, not cell assignment: four quoins hold a fixed five-tile arrangement flush, pressure creeps down until a tile pies out of plane, and a quoin-by-quoin re-key — occasionally a full planer sweep — brings it back, forever.

Rendered on the shadcn default neutral palette with harness-generated props.
